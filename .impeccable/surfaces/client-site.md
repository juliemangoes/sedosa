# Surface brief: client site (landing, booking flow, confirmation, account)

Scope: `/`, the booking flow, `/booking/[token]`, `/account`. Visitor mode: **Persuade** (landing) flowing into a short task (booking).
Audience and job: Sedosa clients, mostly on phones from Instagram, choosing a brow/lash/wax service and an open time. Proof on hand: real menu and prices, policies, two real result photos, the studio portrait, owner-uploaded category photos. No reviews or claims exist; none may be invented.
Constraint: pink stays the studio colour; the chrome logo, black-satin/neon look and playful voice are replaceable. Must feel high class and premium, not white or sterile.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Sedosa is a members'-club powder room you step into: lacquered rose walls, oxblood velvet, brass, and the booking waiting on the vanity like a reservation card. It refuses both category ruts: beige spa minimalism and the black-and-neon club flyer the old site wore.

OWN-WORLD: Lacquer rose fields own whole regions (hero wall, menu room); blush plaster for reading rooms; oxblood for ink, primary buttons and the closing room; brass only as fine rims, numerals and focus. Jost for every role: spaced capitals for the wordmark, medium weight for headings and prices, regular for body, tracked caps for small labels. (Revised at the user's request: the Bodoni serif read poorly.) Tall oval vanity mirrors frame photos with a brass rim. Lacquer sheen, never glitter or neon glow.

STORY: The visitor understands this is a polished studio for brows, lashes and waxing in Covington and Decatur, believes the work is premium from the room itself and the real results, and books an open time within a few taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed lacquer rose wall. SEDOSA set huge in oxblood spaced capitals across the width; the portrait in an oval brass-rimmed mirror overlapping the lower half of the wordmark. A reservation card sits at the bottom of the viewport with the next three open times as tappable slots plus "See all times"; on desktop the card sits right of the mirror. Book is also in the header.

FORM: Powder Room. seed: none (pinned) — the user selected "The Powder Room": "A members'-club powder room: lacquered rose walls, oxblood velvet, brass details. Big Bodoni wordmark, oval vanity-mirror photo frames, and the next open times sitting in the first screen like a reservation card." (The concept roll could not run: the skill launcher was blocked in this environment, so the user chose from three derived directions plus the category standard.)

Photo wall (added at the user's request for more pictures): after the menu, an oxblood wall of brass-rimmed oval, arch and round frames holding the owner's uploaded photos, then the two real results. The closing wall returns to rose.

Signature interaction: choosing a category mirror sends a slow light sheen across its glass while the menu below swaps to that category. Motion grammar: unhurried exponential ease-out, one sheen per choice, content visible by default.
